# as in every reported block, and without it, in the same randomized rounds, so that the difference of the means of
# the twins is the cost of recording the layer timings. Every fork installs the probes; without the profiler they do
# not record, and each costs one volatile read. The telemetry profiler runs in every fork.
# The cells: a read with one of the highest probe rates of RQ1 (JDBC MQ-DTO at fan-out 0: 7 probes in about 0.9 ms;
# JDBC MS-JSON at fan-out 0 has 5 in about 0.64 ms), the shortest edit (JDBC Diff-Merge over REST: 12 probes in
# about 2.1 ms), and the RQ2 edit with the most probes (JPA Multi-Ops over REST: 37 probes in about 6.3 ms).
# The digest in session.properties covers the cell lines only, so comments do not change it.
# Same JMH options and rounds as rq1-rest and rq2-writes,
# on the layout of the campaign: s68 runs the benchmarks and s66 PostgreSQL.
